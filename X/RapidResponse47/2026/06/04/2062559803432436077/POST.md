# "HHS office of the Inspector General... in coordination with the @WHFraudTF, has decertifi...

"HHS office of the Inspector General... in coordination with the @WHFraudTF, has decertified Hawaii’s Medicaid fraud control unit," says @AFergusonFTC.

"For more than a decade, Hawaii’s Medicaid fraud control unit has received millions and millions of dollars to fight fraud and is consistently been one of the lowest performing fraud units in the country... from 2021 to 2025, Hawaii’s Medicaid funding went up 27%. Their Medicaid enrollment went up 40%, and they did not produce a single conviction or obtain a single indictment of a fraudster."
