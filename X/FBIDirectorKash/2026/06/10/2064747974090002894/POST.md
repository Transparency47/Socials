# Great to be with our @FBIDenver team – one of the best offices in the nation leading the f...

Great to be with our @FBIDenver team – one of the best offices in the nation leading the fight to make America safe again. 
 
As of June 2026, the Denver Homeland Security Task Force leads the United States in fentanyl seizures this year with 170.9 kg fentanyl seized, as well as 711.6 kg meth seized – an outstanding representation of this FBI’s work to take deadly drugs off American streets.
 
Through the Violent Criminal Enterprise Task Force, they’ve also had tremendous success taking down violent gangs like Tren de Aragua – including one investigation which led to RICO charges against leaders of TdA for a string of violent crimes in the Denver metro area. The crimes included a jewelry store robbery that netted millions of dollars and even a kidnapping in which a victim was tortured and after his family refused to pay ransom.
 
Thanks to the team for their great work and hosting us.
