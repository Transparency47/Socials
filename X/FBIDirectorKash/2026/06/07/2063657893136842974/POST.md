# Repost of @EpochTimes: The Justice Department announced the results of Operation Spring Cl...

Repost of @EpochTimes:

The Justice Department announced the results of Operation Spring Cleaning, a nationwide FBI-led effort from March 1 to May 31 that resulted in 1,139 arrests and 615 criminal indictments.

Nearly 600 search warrants were executed as federal, state, and local agencies targeted illegal firearms and drugs linked to gang activity.

Authorities seized 984 illegal firearms, including dozens equipped with machine-gun conversion devices and more than 75 stand-alone devices, along with more than 2,700 pounds of narcotics.

These included over 500 kilograms of cocaine, nearly 700 pounds of methamphetamine, more than 550 pounds of marijuana, nearly 50 kilograms of fentanyl, almost 40 kilograms of heroin, over 7 kilograms of crack cocaine, and more than 13,200 MDMA pills.

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche described the operation as part of a commitment to public safety and removing deadly drugs from streets.

FBI Director Kash Patel highlighted the fight against gangs and violent crime, noting it as part of ongoing efforts and the launch of Operation Summer Heat 2.0.

Preliminary data showed a 9.3 percent decrease in violent crime from 2024 to 2025, including an 18.1 percent drop in murders.

The operation continues a series of coordinated federal initiatives, building on last year’s Operation Summer Heat.
