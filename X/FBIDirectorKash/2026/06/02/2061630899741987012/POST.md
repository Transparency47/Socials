# Repost of @FBI: At this year’s Indy 500, @FBIIndianapolis was out in full force, with more...

Repost of @FBI:

At this year’s Indy 500, @FBIIndianapolis was out in full force, with more than 25 personnel supporting security operations and helping safeguard the 350,000 spectators who packed the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.
 
Operational Highlights:

• Responded to 12+ drone incursions throughout race weekend • Our Ground Intercept Teams moved fast - reaching non‑compliant operators within seconds of drone detections across 600+ acres of IMS and surrounding areas • Multiple drones were seized for violating the Temporary Flight Restriction in place for the event
 
Through close coordination with our local, state, and federal partners - including the Speedway Police Department and DHS - we helped ensure a safe, secure, and successful running of one of America’s largest sporting events.

FBI continues to move full throttle with our interagency partners making major US hosted sporting events like F1, the Club World Cup, the Super Bowl, the approaching 2026 FIFA World Cup, and upcoming 2028 Summer Olympics safe and secure for athletes and viewers.
