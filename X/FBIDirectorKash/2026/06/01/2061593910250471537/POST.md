June 1: FBI cracking down on hoax threats during @FIFAWorldCup 
 
With 78 matches in 11 U.S. cities across 39 days, one issue this @FBI is taking incredibly seriously during the tournament is hoax threats. Hoax threats are federal crimes, and for good reason - they sow panic, waste valuable law enforcement resources, and put innocent lives at risk. 
 
Hoax threats will not be tolerated. The FBI and its partners will be rigorous and relentless in tracking down anyone who makes a fake threat at any event on any device during this historic tournament.
 
This is part of our continuing campaign to investigate and raise public awareness of hoax threats. Some FBI cases over the last month alone: 
 
- An 18-year-old in Oregon was arrested and has been federally charged with making at least 17 bomb threats that terrorized Ohio schools and other organizations earlier in the year. 

- A man was arrested in Hungary for causing a “swatting” incident in Rhode Island in 2024, prompting an armed response by local authorities following false claims of the murder of a family. 
 
Remember: A single threat via text, phone call, or social media can land you in jail. Do not make, post, or share hoax threats. If you see or hear about such a threat, report it immediately to 1-800-CALL-FBI or at https://t.co/m55w5cqC7Y
