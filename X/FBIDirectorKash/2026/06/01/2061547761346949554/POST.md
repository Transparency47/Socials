# Rest in Peace, Deputy Sheriff Logan Utt of the Carroll County Sheriff’s Office in Virginia...

Rest in Peace, Deputy Sheriff Logan Utt of the Carroll County Sheriff’s Office in Virginia. End of Watch: May 29, 2026. 

Deputy Sheriff Utt was a military veteran and served with the Carroll County Sheriff’s Office for 2 years and 5 months. He is survived by his wife and two children.

The FBI sends our prayers and condolences to Deputy Sheriff Utt’s family, friends, and colleagues left behind. They will have our full support.
