# Repost of @FBI: Today the FBI officially welcomed the newest class of the FBI Honors Inter...

Repost of @FBI:

Today the FBI officially welcomed the newest class of the FBI Honors Internship Program – an exceptional group of students bringing incredible experience, academic excellence, and a shared commitment to serving America. Throughout the program, this group will gain hands-on experience, contribute to mission-driven work, and learn firsthand how the FBI fulfills its mission to protect the American people. They will get the opportunity to participate in the FBI's record-breaking work happening across the country every day to make communities safer than we've ever been.

We’re thrilled to have them here at the greatest law enforcement agency on earth and witness the impact they will make as the next generation of leaders – welcome team!
