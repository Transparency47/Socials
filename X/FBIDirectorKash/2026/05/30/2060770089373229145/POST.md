A massive child exploitation task force operation in north Texas in March and April resulted in 276 arrests and the rescue of 89 children. The FBI’s Dallas division partnered with the Dallas Police Department and 91 law enforcement agencies for Operation Soteria Shield, involving 197 personnel focused on online sexual exploitation through platforms, social media, and messaging apps.

Investigators targeted suspects in possession, distribution, production, promotion of child sexual abuse material, trafficking, sexual assault, and related offenses while locating exploited children, identifying unknown victims, and connecting them with social services. The generated cases remain active with possible additional charges.

FBI and Fort Worth Police officials highlighted the collaborative impact against child predators, especially during Child Abuse Prevention Month. The public is encouraged to report exploitation, discuss online safety with children, monitor activity, and contact authorities or tips.fbi[dot]gov if needed.

The National Center for Missing and Exploited Children reported reports climbing from 292,951 to 518,720 in the first half of 2025 amid rising online crimes targeting children.
