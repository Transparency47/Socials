# Great to join the Professionalizing Law Enforcement – Community Engagement Training (PLECE...

Great to join the Professionalizing Law Enforcement – Community Engagement Training (PLECET) conference in Dallas Texas with @DAGToddBlanche, Terry Cole @DEAHQ @ATFRCekeda and Director Serralta @USMarshallsHQ – speaking before a group of 600 on the importance of community law enforcement partnerships making America safe again. 

This @FBI has countless stories of working with the public to crush crime – including one recent example where a brave student came forward to report an alleged abuser after an @FBIAlbany presentation on online safety at a local high school. 

8 hours later, a subject was in custody - and that subject was just sentenced to prison last month. 

There are real stories like this every single day across this country making the difference in safer communities. Thank you to the PLECET hosts for having us.
