# Repost of @FBIBoston: There are many unsung heroes at #FBI Boston - employees who perform ...

Repost of @FBIBoston:

There are many unsung heroes at #FBI Boston - employees who perform critical services for own people or those we serve and protect -  but who often fly under the radar.

Members of  FBI Boston's Emergency Medical Support Program, which we commonly refer to as OpMed, are highly trained special agents and employees  who provide everything from medical plans and coverage for high-risk operations, to training in life-saving medical care,  to immediate medical treatment. During a recent training at Fort Devens, these behind-the-scenes photos show the team working alongside our partners in a mock exercise.

We're  grateful for everything they do to keep us safe!
