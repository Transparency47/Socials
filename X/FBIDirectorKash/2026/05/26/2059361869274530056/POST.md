# Repost of @FBI: The true work of this FBI’s historic year in crime reduction are the stori...

Repost of @FBI:

The true work of this FBI’s historic year in crime reduction are the stories behind the record-breaking stats — Today’s story behind the numbers: This FBI worked with local police in Durham, North Carolina, to crush crime in a community who needed it.

FBI and local partners executed a joint op called “Operation Spring Seizure” to proactively address concerns from the community on crime statistics. As a result: we served 58 outstanding warrants, arrested 29 subjects, and seized fentanyl, cocaine, illegal firearms, and more.

Among the individuals arrested were subjects wanted on 17 counts of vehicle breaking and entering, a subject charged with domestic violence, one subject on multiple felony possession charges, and even one subject who had been coercing a minor child into assisting criminal activity.

This FBI is responding to local communities and executing with our partners more quickly than ever before – and results like these happening all over America are the reason why.
