The FBI arrested a man who threatened to kill an ICE officer, his wife, and children during protests at the Delaney Hall detention facility in New Jersey.

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche called the threats a federal crime, vowing arrests and noting the lack of local law enforcement support that enables repeated attacks on facilities.

Protests have continued for days with clashes, as Democratic lawmakers including Reps. Nadler and Goldman, and Sen. Kim, alleged mistreatment and called for the facility’s closure.

DHS dismissed the claims, stating detainees receive meals, medical care, and higher standards than most U.S. prisons, while blaming political stunts for a surge in assaults and threats on officers.

President Trump called the protesters fake and paid for, and DHS Secretary Mullin thanked the FBI for the arrest.
