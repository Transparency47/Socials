# Hawaii police apprehended and arrested 36-year-old Jacob Daniel Baker May 28. He was wante...

Hawaii police apprehended and arrested 36-year-old Jacob Daniel Baker May 28. He was wanted in connection with the murders of three elderly men in Puna in a matter of two days.

🚨ICE Homeland Security Investigations special agents assisted with the extensive, multi-agency manhunt.

🔎This investigation is ongoing and local police have asked anyone with information to call the department’s non-emergency number at 808-935-3311. Alternately, anonymous tips can be made by calling Crime Stoppers at 808-961-8300.
