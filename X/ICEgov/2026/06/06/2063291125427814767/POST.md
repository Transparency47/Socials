# ⚽ Counterfeit merchandise undermines legitimate business, can be dangerous to consumers, a...

⚽ Counterfeit merchandise undermines legitimate business, can be dangerous to consumers, and often funds other illicit activities like drug and weapons trafficking.

The ICE HSI-led @IPRCenter is the U.S.’s response to intellectual property theft and international trade law violations.

The IPR Center partners with the #FIFAWorldCup2026 and all major U.S. sports events as part of Operation Team Player to ensure a safer and more enjoyable time for fans and players.
