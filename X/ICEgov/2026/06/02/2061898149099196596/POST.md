# 27-year-old Nicholas Matthew Scelf from Brooklyn, NY, was charged Monday with threatening ...

27-year-old Nicholas Matthew Scelf from Brooklyn, NY, was charged Monday with threatening to assault and murder an ICE officer and his family outside the Delaney Hall detention facility May 27.

If convicted, the threat charge carries a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison and a $250,000 fine.

Threatening or assaulting federal officers is not a badge of honor, nor is it a tool for change. 

It’s a federal crime against fellow Americans serving our communities that endangers everyone, including innocent bystanders.
