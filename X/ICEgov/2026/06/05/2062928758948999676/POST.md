# Mosaih Omar Wright aka Kevin Foster, of Jamaica, entered the U.S. in Miami, Fla., in 1995 ...

Mosaih Omar Wright aka Kevin Foster, of Jamaica, entered the U.S. in Miami, Fla., in 1995 as a stowaway and was previously deported as a murderer. 

🔎Aliens arriving as stowaways are not eligible for admission under 8 U.S. Code § 1225 and he was issued a final order of removal in 1997, but before immigration officials were able to remove him, he ended up convicted of murder in Minnesota in relation to a drug trafficking offense and sentenced to serve 192 months in prison. 

✈️Finally, in 2013, ICE deported him via ICE Air after he was released from prison. But he snuck back in and ended up back in prison. ICE took custody of him May 22 from a federal prison in New York.

🛑ICE will deport him again, and this time, thanks to President Trump’s commitment to border security, he won’t be able to sneak back in.
