# Repost of @AliBradleyTV: ICYMI: A @DHSgov Inspector General report found no evidence that ...

Repost of @AliBradleyTV:

ICYMI: A @DHSgov Inspector General report found no evidence that detainees at @ICEgov Washington Field Office holding facility in Chantilly, Virginia, were denied food or drinking water or housed in overcrowded conditions during a 2025 inspection period.

Investigators found ICE staff met requirements for meals, water, facility capacity, and average detention times—and in some cases exceeded standards by providing access to showers and telephones.

However, the report did identify instances where some detainees were held beyond the agency’s 72-hour detention requirement.
