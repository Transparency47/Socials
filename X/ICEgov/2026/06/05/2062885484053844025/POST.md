48 ARRESTED IN MASSIVE TEXAS METH STING

Nearly 80 pounds of methamphetamine, 300 grams of cocaine and 10 firearms were also seized as ICE HSI with its state, local and federal partners dismantled a large-scale methamphetamine trafficking network in the Concho Valley region of Texas.

“What we face today is more complex, more violent and more interconnected than ever before,” said ICE HSI San Angelo Resident Agent in Charge Juan Lujan. “@HSTFNCC comes here as a significant federal partner to contribute to the incredible work already being done on the ground by our state and local law enforcement partners.”
