# Parents detained by ICE choose whether they want to name a guardian for their child. Many ...

Parents detained by ICE choose whether they want to name a guardian for their child. Many parents, understandably, want their children by their side.

Unaccompanied alien children discovered in the United States without a legal guardian, on the other hand, ARE NOT HOUSED in ICE detention facilities — they are transferred into the care of the Office of Refugee Resettlement, which is an agency within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, which facilitates the safe custody of unaccompanied minors.
