# Vidal George-Jimenz, of Honduras, just finished up a 2012 rape conviction sentence that fo...

Vidal George-Jimenz, of Honduras, just finished up a 2012 rape conviction sentence that followed his arrest by police in Portland on charges of sodomy, sexual abuse, sexual penetration and strangulation.

Unfortunately, due to sanctuary policies in Portland, officials refused to honor an ICE detainer, and he was released back into the community after serving his sentence.

ICE Portland Fugitive Operations had to find him in the community at-large. Officers arrested him May 21 at his residence.
