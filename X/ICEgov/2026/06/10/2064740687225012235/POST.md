# 48 illegal aliens were detained and two business managers arrested June 3 as part of years...

48 illegal aliens were detained and two business managers arrested June 3 as part of years-long and ongoing fraudulent identification case in South Carolina.

“The crimes uncovered … are not victimless,” said Mark M. Zito, Special Agent in Charge of ICE Homeland Security Investigations in North and South Carolina. “When illegal aliens use stolen identities and forged documents, they victimize law-abiding citizens whose personal information is misused. These actions also enable businesses to profit from illegal labor at the expense of honest workers and employers.”

ICE officers and special agents are proud to assist South Carolina state and local law enforcement in the case.
