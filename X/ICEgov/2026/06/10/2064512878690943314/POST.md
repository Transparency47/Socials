# ICE issued an immigration detainer with the Alachua County Jail asking them to contact ICE...

ICE issued an immigration detainer with the Alachua County Jail asking them to contact ICE if they are going to release Gurpinder Singh on bond — that way ICE can assume custody of him while he awaits justice in this case.

🔎Singh is an Indian national arrested for allegedly trying to scam her out of nearly $500K worth of property. He entered the United States illegally at a currently unknown time and place.
