# ICE and @DHSgov released the first VOICE and DHS VINE data reports to showcase the agency’...

ICE and @DHSgov released the first VOICE and DHS VINE data reports to showcase the agency’s support for victims of alien crime.

“@POTUS Trump has made it clear that justice for victims of illegal alien crime is a top priority,” said acting ICE Director David J. Venturella. “These reports show DHS is backing up that commitment with action — giving victims and their families a direct line to information, support and accountability when it comes to criminal aliens in our custody.”
