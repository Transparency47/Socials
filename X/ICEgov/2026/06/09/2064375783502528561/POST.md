# Thanks to a 287(g) partnership, the Mongomery County Sheriff’s Office quickly confirmed th...

Thanks to a 287(g) partnership, the Mongomery County Sheriff’s Office quickly confirmed the immigration status of Juan Ayala Montero, a 60-year-old criminal illegal alien from Mexico, who was arrested after allegedly opening fire on his neighbors over Memorial Day weekend. After confirming, they were able to lodge an immigration detainer and prevent him from being released if he were granted bond.
