# Repost of @Interior: As America counts down to her 250th Anniversary, history isn’t just b...

Repost of @Interior:

As America counts down to her 250th Anniversary, history isn’t just being remembered. It’s flowing again.

This week’s ribbon cuttings marked a triumphant return, with fountains and statues sparkling and waters flowing once again, each one a celebration of history, community, and beauty restored.

This is @POTUS's vision in action. Through the Making DC Safe & Beautiful initiative, we are restoring the heart of our nation’s capital and putting America on the path to having the most beautiful capital city in the world.

Thanks to the National Park Service experts for their care of our national spaces.

And here’s to the water flowing again.
