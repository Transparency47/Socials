# Repost of @SecRollins: New SNAP stocking standards are raising the bar for real nutrition....

Repost of @SecRollins:

New SNAP stocking standards are raising the bar for real nutrition.

We’re requiring more than 250,000 retailers to stock, at minimum, 28 varieties of healthy staples. more than doubling the old minimum of just 12. That means 7 varieties per category.

🥩 More real meat, poultry & fish
🥛 More dairy
🍞 More nutritious breads & grains
🥦 More fruits & vegetables

No more loopholes letting jelly count as fruit or processed snacks stand in for real food!

Better nutrition for families. Stronger protection for taxpayers against waste and fraud.

This is MAHA in action!
