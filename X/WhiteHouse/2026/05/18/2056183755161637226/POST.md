# Repost of @SecRollins: 🚨Trump to China will go down as a truly HISTORIC state visit! CONFI...

Repost of @SecRollins:

🚨Trump to China will go down as a truly HISTORIC state visit! 

CONFIRMED WINS FOR U.S. FARMERS: 

1. $17B in NEW purchase commitments, in addition to the 25MMT soybean commitment.

2. U.S. poultry will be back on the table in China, as they are resuming U.S. imports.

3. U.S. beef is also on the menu, as China is renewing licenses for more than 400 beef facilities, and is adding new listings. This was the #1 “ask” by our ranchers who want to sell many of the “leftover” cuts of the animal (hides, skins, tongues, tendons, tripe, etc) for additional value. 

As the United States continues to diversify our overall exports this agreement provides stability in one of our largest markets.
