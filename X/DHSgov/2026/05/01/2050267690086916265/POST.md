# Repost of @USBPChief: Agents from the Nogales Border Patrol Station apprehended 13 illegal...

Repost of @USBPChief:

Agents from the Nogales Border Patrol Station apprehended 13 illegal aliens attempting to cross illegally in the remote Sonoran Desert. Among them, one Mexican national was identified as having ties to the Sinaloa Cartel.
 
Our message is clear: If you cross illegally, you will be arrested, prosecuted, and deported. We remain committed to using every available resource to protect our nation’s borders.
