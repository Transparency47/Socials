# Repost of @USCG: “It’s one team, one fight.” On Tuesday, @SecWar visited @USCG HQ, the 1st...

Repost of @USCG:

“It’s one team, one fight.”

On Tuesday, @SecWar visited @USCG HQ, the 1st such visit by a Secretary of War in our 236-year history. Alongside @SecMullinDHS, Secretary Hegseth spoke with our personnel regarding the Coast Guard’s impact within the Joint Force, unique authorities and capabilities, and historic operations in defense of America. 

@USCG is proud to serve alongside our fellow armed services, and grateful for the inspiring remarks by Secretary Hegseth.
