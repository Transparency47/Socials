Repost of @USBPChiefELC:

Established on May 28, 1924, the USBP was born from a critical need to secure our expanding borders.

From its early days, with agents often providing their own horses and navigating vast, rugged terrains, the Border Patrol's mission has continuously evolved. Through eras of Prohibition, shifting immigration policies, and the complexities of national security, agents have adapted, utilizing new technologies and strategies to meet the challenges of their time. This rich history is a testament to a century of unwavering dedication and resilience.

Today, that legacy lives on through the brave men and women who stand guard 24/7. Their commitment ensures the integrity of our borders and the safety of our communities. As we reflect on 102 years of service, we honor every agent who has contributed to this vital mission. Happy 102nd Birthday, U.S. Border Patrol!
