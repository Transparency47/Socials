# This is nothing more than already debunked nonsense. NO detainees were beaten or attacked ...

This is nothing more than already debunked nonsense. NO detainees were beaten or attacked by ICE agents. 

On May 28, 2026, staff responded to a physical fight involving detainees at Delaney Hall. In accordance with established ICE policies and their training, staff used the minimum amount of force to safely deescalate the situation. 

Following the incident, ALL affected detainees were promptly evaluated by on-site medical personnel and were cleared with no serious injuries.
