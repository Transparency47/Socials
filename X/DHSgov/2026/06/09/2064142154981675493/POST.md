AMERICAN HERO.
 
In April, @CBP Port of Detroit officer Sergiu Botezan was walking his dog when he noticed a distressed child riding a bike in his neighborhood.
 
The boy told Botezan he was lost and asked for help to contact his mother. Botezan called his mother, and waited with the child until she arrived.
 
Our officers truly are the best of the best. Thank you, Officer Botezan, for helping ensure a safe reunion between this child and his mother.
