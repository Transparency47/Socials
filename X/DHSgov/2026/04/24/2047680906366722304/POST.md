PROTECTING OUR NATION. 

@USCGSoutheast personnel at Base Miami Beach offloaded approximately 2,570 pounds of cocaine worth more than $19.3 MILLION and transferred six suspected drug smugglers to federal authorities. 

By keeping these drugs off our streets and out of our communities, these heroes saved countless American lives.
