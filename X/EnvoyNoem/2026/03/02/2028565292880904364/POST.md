# Today, I had the privilege of joining the Western States Sheriff’s Association in Reno, NV...

Today, I had the privilege of joining the Western States Sheriff’s Association in Reno, NV. I am confident that increased cooperation among ICE, DHS, and local law enforcement will help accomplish what the American people voted for under President Trump — safer communities and restored law and order.

We have accomplished so much in the last year, including a 125-year low in our murder rate, and with the support of state and local law enforcement leaders, the next three years will be even better.

I look forward to our continued partnership to make America safe again.
